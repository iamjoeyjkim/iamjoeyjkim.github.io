Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2025

Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how ICD10 Navigator handles your data.

✓ Data Collection

ICD10 Navigator does NOT collect, transmit, or share any personal information. All data remains on your device.

✓ Local Storage Only

Your favorites and search history are stored locally on your device using iOS UserDefaults. This data never leaves your device.

✓ No Analytics

We do not use any analytics, tracking, or telemetry services. Your usage of this app is completely private.

✓ No Internet Required

This app functions 100% offline. No internet connection is required or used for core functionality.

✓ No Third Parties

We do not share data with any third parties because we don't collect any data in the first place.

✓ Your Control

You can clear your search history and favorites at any time from within the app. Uninstalling the app will permanently delete all locally stored data.
